## 1. Goal and evidenced gap

Make a `ci_watch` follow GitHub Actions to its final result while the owning agent **and all frontends are idle**. Deliver one durable notify-only result before any subsequent turn. Fix per-turn `CiWatchService`/notification-service ownership and cross-instance races, and reconcile a watch persisted before its M001 subscription. No model turn is required or permitted in C001.

The previous M004 fixture drove `observe_once` manually; live advancement only occurred in `src/tool/ci_watch.rs::status` and `src/agent/follow_up.rs::inject_pending_notifications` at turn start. `src/agent/turn_runtime.rs` constructs fresh `Arc<CiWatchService>` and `Arc<JobNotificationService>` for every turn; `JobScheduler::set_job_notification_service` is overwritten on each turn. `ci_watch.rs::in_flight` is process-memory within one service, not a shared daemon lease. `ci_watch.rs::create_watch` writes its `ci_watch` row before subscribing to `ciwatch:<watch_id>`. These are the concrete defects C001 must address.

## 2. Owner/contract before code

- One daemon-scoped `Arc<JobNotificationService>` and `Arc<CiWatchService>` for its lifetime, created in canonical `CoreRuntimeDeps`/`CoreDaemon` startup and **injected** into turn runtime and tool factory. The implementation agent must examine existing `CoreDaemon`, `CoreRuntimeDeps`, scheduler startup, session runtime and `TurnRuntime` fixtures before selecting the narrowest wiring seam. In-memory/headless modes either share one lifecycle owner or explicitly fail idle watch support; never claim process-independent persistence when no pool exists.
- One canonical daemon-managed wake/coordinator for the **earliest due** row(s). Prefer hooking existing scheduler maintenance/reconciliation or its bounded due-task infrastructure; if that cannot represent a finite adaptive network observation, use one daemon-lifecycle-owned cancellable timer/wakeup that drives **existing** admission and durable watch state. Do not build another generic scheduling engine or store, or one `tokio::spawn`/interval per watch. Document exact ownership versus `ScheduleStore` and `JobScheduler`; keep the watch's remote wait outside process/CPU permits.
- Each finite observation must be subject to a global cap, per-host/account rate budget, max active requests and bounded timeout, with fair ordering across sessions. Durable `next_check_at`, TTL, backoff and expiry govern selection; an empty due queue must sleep without busy polling. A watch expiry must generate one attention notice even when its session never returns.
- For multi-instance/daemon-restart safety, use a **persisted CAS claim/lease or generation fence** on each observation so two independent `CiWatchService` handles cannot simultaneously poll/update the same watch or send two terminal outcomes. Process-local locks remain an optimization only. Recover expired claims and unfinished observations without treating response ambiguity as successful CI.
- Watch acceptance and subscription/lease must be transactional or explicitly reconciled: crash between row write and subscription; subscription failure on create; duplicate accepted invocation with mismatched payload; completed run before first observation; restart during terminal-before-notice all result in either a valid, owned watch+notice or an explicit terminal attention state. No orphan forever in `Pending`.
- Default delivery is `notify_only`. Persist the authoritative terminal result and M001 notice/session attention entry **independent of AgentLoop start**. AgentLoop may inject a system-role summary at a safe boundary later, but cannot be the only producer of the durable event. Do not inject external GitHub text as trusted instructions.
- Auth remains bound to immutable host session/principal/workspace/repo; a guessed handle, second session, cancelled owner or revoked principal cannot inspect/cancel or wake unrelated work. Credential refs only, no token storage. Preserve URL allowlist, redirect and response caps, rate-limit/ETag behavior, no remote workflow cancellation.

## 3. Ordered implementation packages

A. **Red tests and contract diagram.** Add deterministic fail-first tests for: (i) dormant watch completes without further agent/model/user/tool activity, (ii) watch times out without activity and raises attention, (iii) concurrent turns/daemon-scoped services cannot double-poll, (iv) persisted row but absent M001 subscription after injected failure, (v) duplicate external completion and shutdown/restart. Freeze the timer/admission authority decision in `architecture/scheduler.md`.

B. **Promote service ownership.** Move notifier and watcher construction from `src/agent/turn_runtime.rs` into canonical daemon lifetime. Inject shared handles into each `TurnRunInput` / model tool factory and scheduler with one authoritative pointer. Avoid broad lifecycle refactor or unrelated M017 Tool Program changes. Prove two turns share pointer/lease identity and closing one turn leaves watchers live.

C. **Finite due observation.** Add a daemon-owned driver that queries persisted due rows globally (`state IN (pending, observing)`, `next_check_at <= now`, bounded LIMIT, stable due/order/tie break) with fair per-session/host scheduling; a single earliest-due wake and response to creation/updates; finite bounded request work dispatched through existing scheduler admission/daemon runtime. Idle scans must not depend on in-memory `watches` from one session. Shutdown cancels in-flight observation safely, retains persisted lease and next due; startup recovers and schedules without storm. No per-watch long-lived worker.

D. **Atomic claim and generation.** Extend `ci_watch` schema in a narrowly additive migration only as needed (`claim_owner`, `claim_until`, `generation`/`attempt` or equivalent), with CAS update and explicit lease state transitions. DB/store holds the cross-service authority, not `in_flight: HashMap`. A slow old holder must fail its stale final update after lease generation changes. Bound attempts/backoff; no double notices or overlapping HTTP.

E. **Watch + subscription repair.** Commit watch creation and M001 notification subscription/optional future lease in a consistent store boundary, or implement persisted `registration_pending`/reconciler with deterministic fingerprint and explicit retry/attention. `persist` before `subscribe_with_options` is not sufficient by itself. Preserve idempotent same invocation and conflict on different request; avoid foreground test/bash changes.

F. **Independent terminal publisher.** Drive `JobNotificationService::record_terminal_for_watch`, session event `append_idempotent`, optional frontend notification router/projection, and ack/replay from daemon-authoritative terminal transition rather than needing a turn boundary. If the existing M001 recovery helper embeds `AgentLoop`/system-message injection, factor a non-model durable publish step without changing M017 semantics. Preserve safe boundary injection for active turn as additional view, not sole delivery. Ensure failed notify insert never retries remote CI process.

G. **Docs, diagnostics, closure.** Update `architecture/jobs.md`, `architecture/scheduler.md`, `architecture/authorization.md` and operator config as affected. Add counts for active/overdue/leased/last-observed/expired/attention, no secret logs. Preserve `ci_watch` tool schema and M004 contract unless a documented compatible addition is necessary. Update active registry and create `plans/closure/async-background-jobs-corrective/001-status.md`.

## 4. Strict acceptance and tests

1. **Zero-turn positive:** Start an actual daemon lifecycle with SQLite and loopback fake GitHub run. Register watch, end owning turn/close TUI. Fake remote run transitions queued -> running -> completed. Without calling `ci_watch.status`, `advance_due_watches`, `AgentLoop.run` or manual `observe_once` from the test harness, the daemon-owned trigger reaches terminal and commits **one** session event/notice before a fake-clock deadline. No model calls and no indefinite worker/permit.
2. **No-turn expiry:** Frozen clock advances past TTL with no client activity. Exactly one `expired` attention event; no false `CI failed`, no remote workflow cancellation.
3. **Cross-service:** Two concurrent turns and two service instances share persisted watch identity; barrier releases 50 contenders, at most one admitted remote read per due occurrence/lease. Stale generation cannot overwrite newer terminal state.
4. **Crash interleavings:** Terminate/recreate daemon immediately (a) after watch insert but before subscription, (b) after subscription but before driver registration, (c) after due-claim, (d) after remote terminal persisted and before notice/event, (e) after append but before ack. Recover with 1 watch and 1 actionable notice, without duplicate process/model work.
5. **Rate/security:** 401/403/404/429/503, ETag, Retry-After, malformed/large body, redirected host/SSRF and foreign session fail closed; budget and TLS limits preserved. Two projects cannot starve one another indefinitely.
6. **Compatibility:** M001/M002/M003/M004 tests remain green, especially `tests/ci_watch_m004_completion.rs`, `tests/job_notification_m001_contract.rs`, `tests/tool_program_m017_notification_confirmation.rs`; `JobScheduler` canonical authority unchanged.

## 5. Stop / deviation / closure

Stop if daemon due progression still depends on any interactive/model call, if per-turn construction remains a source of two authoritative watchers, if subscription failures can leave silently unnotifiable active watches, or if a new independent scheduler is required. Record exact gaps and a new bounded corrective, do not close C001. Do not mark C002 ready until strict C001 status and daemon-level idle evidence are committed. Existing M004 historical record stays unchanged.
