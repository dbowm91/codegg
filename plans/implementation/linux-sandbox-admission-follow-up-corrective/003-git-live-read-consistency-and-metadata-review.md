## Defect and previous evidence gap

The former trusted Git approach prepares immutable private ODB snapshots but cold fragmented-object preparation failed C010's performance budget (Btrfs p95 1,109.391 ms versus 1,000 ms). In-process locks cannot exclude arbitrary external writers; C012 correctly rejected treating a live read-only mount as an immutable snapshot. The test-only contained live-read pilot is faster but not generation-consistent. ADR-0044 C006 review identified that receipt wording alone is inadequate: consumers could silently use a torn live read for diff/publish/merge-base. `filter.*` and `include.path` trust/mediation are incomplete. C007 **already** implemented private-index staging, `index.lock` CAS, durable journal, recovery and actual M004/M005 hosted staging; do not duplicate or misstate that boundary.

Prior tests primarily qualified isolated candidate behavior/cost and private-index publication, **not** typed consumer-level rejection of weaker consistency or the full hostile executable/configuration matrix under actual production dispatch.

## Objective, ownership and non-goals

Produce a precise typed **consistency/authority contract** for Git results and a maintainer-ready ADR-0044 amendment/decision packet, with fail-closed consumer tests and hostile-metadata negatives. Keep immutable snapshot reads the ordinary route. The typed distinction must reflect at least (a) immutable captured generation, (b) captured but possibly stale generation/cache result, and (c) contained live mutable read that may be torn or invalidated by outside writers. Do not turn test-only live backend on in shipped binary, accept ADR-0044 by changing its status, auto-relax `CleanSmudgeFilter`/`RepositoryAlias`, or reopen remote Git C003/M006.
